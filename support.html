Common questions

  • Does Alchemy Focus require an account?
    No. The app does not require account registration or login.
  • Where is my data stored?
    Your focus records, projects, circle configurations, names, descriptions, statistics, and settings are stored primarily on your device.
  • How can I remove my data?
    Use the data deletion/reset options available inside the app. Removing the app from the device may also remove locally stored app data, subject to iOS behavior and any backups you maintain.
  • What should I include when reporting a bug?
    Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Alchemy Focus version, what you were doing, and the steps that reproduce the issue.

常见问题

  • 是否需要注册账号？

    可使用 App 内提供的数据删除/重置功能。删除 App 通常也会移除本地 App 数据，具体取决于 iOS 行为以及你自己的备份。

    请提供 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、炼金专注版本、发生问题时正在进行的操作，以及可复现步骤。
